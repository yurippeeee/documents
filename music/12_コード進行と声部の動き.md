## 1. 定番の進行を機能で読む

```play
label: test A chords+bass
chords: C:2 G:2 Am:2 Em:2 F:2 C:2 F:2 G:2
key: C
bass: on
show: chords kbd staff
```

```play
label: test B 4 voices whole
v1: E4:4 F4:4 D4:4 E4:4
v2: C4:4 C4:4 B3:4 C4:4
v3: G3:4 A3:4 G3:4 G3:4
v4: C3:4 F2:4 G2:4 C3:4
show: kbd staff
```

```play
label: test C seq chords
notes: [C3 G3 C4 E4]:2 [F2 A3 C4 F4]:2 [G2 G3 B3 D4]:2 [C3 G3 C4 E4]:2
mode: seq
show: kbd staff
```

```play
label: test D slash
chords: C:2 G/B:2 Am:2 Em/G:2 F:2 C/E:2 F:2 G:2
key: C
bass: on
show: chords kbd staff
```

```play
label: test E 4 voices half
v1: E4:2 F4:2 D4:2 E4:2
v2: C4:2 C4:2 B3:2 C4:2
v3: G3:2 A3:2 G3:2 G3:2
v4: C3:2 F2:2 G2:2 C3:2
show: kbd staff
```
